What practical insights did you gain about robot motion or sensing from the implementation?*

>>> ANSWER: I learned that motion uncertainty accumulates rapidly over distance — even small errors in wheel odometry lead to significant drift, which is why ABB's mobile robots use frequent localization corrections via reflectors and LiDAR. I also understood how the sensor model's four components (hit, short, max, random) elegantly handle real factory scenarios: a forklift suddenly crossing the path (short reading), long open aisles (max-range), and sensor noise from reflective metal surfaces common in industrial environments. This insight explains why ABB combines multiple sensing modalities rather than relying on a single sensor.
